# Visual review — Stage 1 FAIL / no integration

**Does this proposal make Stack & Survive meaningfully more game-like? NO —
current custom art is more coherent.** This rejects this specific selection,
normalization and additive composition, not all future uses of external assets.
It does not establish that a professionally authored replacement hall would fail.

## Evidence and method

Eighteen matched current/proposal pairs, six scenes at each of 1440×900,
1920×1080 and 844×390. See `comparison/captures.json` for image hashes and
the actual tick/state/camera/targets/flows, `comparison/metrics.json` for pixel
differences, and `comparison/side-by-side/` for labelled full-size comparisons.
They are real local Phaser frames with production V3 art and QA manual stepping,
not deployed Pages captures, not concept art, and not human game-feel validation.
Reduced motion makes traffic representative markers stable within pairs; animated
completion timing can still differ. Existing tutorial overlays are not hidden.

The initial capture harness mistakenly captured countdown and was corrected:
final captures assert positive operating tick and actual construction/active/
overcapacity states. That initial output is not used as evidence. State pairs
assert unchanged tick/resource states/camera/world nodes/interaction targets/flows.
Cross-resolution tick equality was not asserted and is not claimed.

## Ten review questions

| Question | Answer anchored to evidence |
|---|---|
| More like a game? | Not materially. `1440-cache-edge-active` already has dimensional distinctive heroes; four stock consoles add bulk rather than a more convincing hall. |
| Architecture easier to read? | Essentially unchanged. Hero positions/routes are identical; the additive environment contributes no new architectural meaning. |
| Core facilities distinguishable? | Yes in active/pressure pairs. Intake chevron, Edge gantry, App modules, green Cache fins, SQL ring remain visible. |
| Traffic visible? | Browse/order markers and App-pressure queue remain distinct in captured normal/pressure/active states. Bot-filter and cache-hit/miss motion were not separately verified; do not generalize this to every flow state. |
| Interactive targets obvious? | Empty Cache/Edge and App bays remain visible; construction and two active modules are shown. Hover/selection/focus were not captured, so interaction acceptance remains incomplete. |
| Background competing? | Yes. The brighter grey consoles at the top and oversized foreground valve in `844-fit` attract attention despite being noninteractive. |
| More depth? | Some local shading/contact depth, especially utility valve and rail. Floating/disconnected pieces and clipped peripheral forms prevent coherent room depth. |
| Still a data center? | Mostly because existing racks/heroes remain. The new consoles and hollow factory machine shells do not improve that reading. |
| Coherent, not asset-flipped? | Fails. Soft rounded grey models contrast with blue, crisp, densely lined V3 facilities. Desaturation alone does not unify style. |
| Pipeline worth it? | Not for this output. Fifteen source models, Blender calibration and provenance maintenance yield mostly decorative density. |

## Scene observations

- **Normal:** current already separates heroes and peripheral racks. Imported
  consoles resemble terminals, not the needed server racks. Rectangular wall
  segments sit as isolated objects rather than an indoor boundary.
- **Construction:** real Cache/Edge construction and App bay expansion are
  retained. Added props do not improve construction legibility or target meaning.
- **Cache+Edge active:** topology and distinctive service silhouettes remain;
  the best part of the proposal is still the existing custom midground.
- **Pressure:** real App overcapacity and loss/queue markers remain readable.
  No evidence that imported machinery explains the pressure better.
- **Fit:** strongest view of all added elements, but most clearly exposes a
  scatter of unrelated equipment and mismatched scale. Original racks provide
  the data-center identity; external substitutes do not.
- **Close:** peripheral walls/pipes are clipped by the unchanged camera. We did
  not alter camera behavior to flatter the experiment. Their benefit is small.
- **Mobile:** `844-fit` retains core topology, but tutorial/HUD already occupy
  much of the viewport. Additional foreground machinery consumes scarce visual
  attention. No mobile device usability or hardware performance claim is made.

## Failure causes and possible next experiment

1. No literal data-center rack or HVAC in the selected set; console/machine
   substitutions do not meet those roles. Keep existing custom racks/cooling.
2. Shared material edits remove stock colors but do not match edge treatment,
   contrast or actual operational detail. Several flat templates face away or
   contribute little visible geometry in the chosen orientation.
3. Per-object bound normalization does not preserve physical source scale.
   The valve and consoles need explicit dimensions and assembled utility runs.
4. An additive overlay cannot remove clashes in the existing environment and is
   weaker evidence than a fully authored replacement hall. That larger change
   is not justified by these initial candidates.

Keep the source experiment for traceability. If pursued later, test **only pipe,
elbow, valve and rail**, connected into a low-contrast utility assembly, or source
an actually verified server-rack/HVAC pack. Do not replace heroes. A new proposal
needs a fresh comparison, all traffic/interaction states and human review before
an integration change. No INTEGRATION_PLAN is authorized by this FAIL.
