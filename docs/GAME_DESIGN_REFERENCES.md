# Game design references

Design commentary only; not implemented feature claims. Stack & Survive studies general patterns that can support experimentation, mastery and short replayable architecture decisions. The intended product remains independent.

| Reference | Pattern considered | Proposed application |
|---|---|---|
| Last War: Survival | Low-friction entry and clear next action | Understandable first interaction with deeper decisions later |
| Mini Metro | Increasing demand under resource constraints | Visible workload and pressure requiring adaptation |
| Bloons TD 6 | Different builds for comparable challenges | Calibrate multiple architecture strategies rather than a single answer |
| Slay the Spire | Repeatable runs, modifiers and experimentation | Versioned challenges and alternative architecture attempts |
| Vampire Survivors | Short decision/feedback cadence and visible growth | Show actual infrastructure activation and changed flow |
| Survivor.io | Simple controls under escalating pressure | Keep each decision accessible while constraints change |

These are project design interpretations, not an exhaustive or authoritative analysis of those products. No proprietary implementation or exact level/UX structure is adopted.

## Patterns, not copies

Study short action-to-feedback loops, escalating constraints, meaningful alternatives, personal bests and deterministic challenge conditions. Adapt them to cloud architecture with testable costs and consequences. Do not reproduce source code, logos, screenshots, artwork, UI assets, characters, audio, game-specific terminology or proprietary level designs.

Same workload, different architectures, different outcomes is an intended product principle; present strategy success as measured evidence, not borrowed credibility. Current and future loops are distinguished in [Replayability Design](REPLAYABILITY_DESIGN.md).
